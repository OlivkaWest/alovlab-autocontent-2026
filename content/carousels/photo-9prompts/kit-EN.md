## SLIDES 2–10 (number · headline · PROMPT · "Next →")

**01 · SHOT ON DSLR (NO PHOTOGRAPHER)**
Turn my photo into a professional DSLR portrait. {LOCK} Place me in [LOCATION]. Realistic lighting, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, soft separation from the background, professional composition. It must look like a real camera shot, not an AI generation.

**02 · STRAIGHT OUT OF A MOVIE**
Turn my photo into a cinematic film still. Place me in [SCENE]. {LOCK} Dramatic but realistic lighting, natural shadows, shallow depth of field, soft background, expensive cinematic composition and believable color grade. Keep textures natural. Like a frame from a high-end film.

**03 · LUXURY INSTAGRAM SHOOT**
Turn my photo into a premium lifestyle shot for a strong personal brand. Place me in [LOCATION], dress me in [OUTFIT]. {LOCK} Complex natural light, relaxed confident pose, expensive composition, shallow depth of field, natural skin. Magazine-level and expensive, no plastic retouch.

**04 · TRAVEL SHOT, NO TRIP**
Create a professional travel shot of me in [LOCATION]. {LOCK} Blend me into the scene realistically: light, shadows, weather, perspective, reflections, outfit and surroundings must match. Lively travel composition, natural colors, real depth. Like a photographer actually shot me there.

**05 · MAGAZINE COVER LOOK**
Turn my photo into a premium editorial fashion portrait. Pick [OUTFIT / STYLE], a confident magazine pose, pro studio light, natural skin, carefully worked shadows and expensive composition. {LOCK} Expressive but realistic color grade. Like a shoot for a major magazine.

**06 · SAVE AN OLD PHOTO**
Restore this old photo while keeping the real person and features. Remove damage, noise, blur and defects. Rebuild natural skin tone, facial detail, clothing, background and light. {LOCK} Make it look shot today on a modern professional camera.

**07 · SWAP THE LOCATION**
Keep me fully recognizable but replace the surroundings with [LOCATION]. {LOCK} Match light direction, shadows, reflections, perspective, atmosphere and how the outfit interacts with the new space. Like a real photo taken right there.

**08 · PRO BUSINESS PORTRAIT**
Turn my photo into a professional business portrait for a website, LinkedIn or personal brand. {LOCK} Clean professional lighting, confident but natural pose, natural skin, sharp facial detail, clean separation from the background. No heavy retouch. Like a real business shoot.

**09 · GOLDEN HOUR SHOOT**
Turn my photo into a golden hour shoot. Place me in [LOCATION]. {LOCK} Low warm sunlight, soft golden highlights, realistic long shadows, warm tones. Light must match the sun position and scene geometry. A real sunset shot, not a filter.
