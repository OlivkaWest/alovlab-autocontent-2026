Three checks I run before I use an AI answer for real work.

One. If there's a primary source, I open it myself — I don't trust the summary.

Two. I take one specific claim from the answer and check that one, not the whole text at once.

[Scene 5 — non-avatar: one phrase highlights, a source opens next to it]

What I don't do: ask the same model again. That's the same source, not a check.

Three checks. Not "I trust it" — "I used it."

What this looks like on a real example — the guide is in Telegram.
