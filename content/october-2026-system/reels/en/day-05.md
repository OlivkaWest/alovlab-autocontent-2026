One question. Two different answers. Not a bug.

[Scene 2 — non-avatar: same prompt typed twice, two different answers appear side by side]

The model doesn't recall a stored answer. It builds one each time, slightly differently.

Both answers can be equally correct. Just different paths to the same result.

Here's what I do with that: I don't treat the first answer as the only one. I ask for two or three
and pick.

[Scene 5 — non-avatar: three answers line up, one gets highlighted as the pick]

Especially for anything going to a client. One version is one attempt, not a fact.

Try it on your next prompt: ask for three, not one.

How to build this into a habit — the guide is in Telegram.
