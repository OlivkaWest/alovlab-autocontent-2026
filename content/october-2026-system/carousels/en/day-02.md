## Slide text (English)
1. Cover: "A PROMPT WITH NO ROLE IS A LETTER WITH NO ADDRESS" / "Walked through on a real client proposal."
2. Problem: "You ask ChatGPT to 'write a client proposal.' You get something that fits any client — and really fits none."
3. Turn: "No role in the prompt. Like a letter with no name on it. It arrives. It doesn't land."
4. Role: "Role isn't 'act as a professional copywriter.' It's who you are in THIS task." Example: "I'm a freelance designer writing to a small coffee shop owner."
5. Context: "Context is what the client already knows — and doesn't." Example: "Saw my work on Instagram, doesn't know my pricing or timeline."
6. Constraints: "What's off the table." Example: "Under 150 words, no corporate tone, don't mention competitors."
7. Format: "What the result should look like." Example: "Three short paragraphs, one concrete next step."
8. Full example (copyable prompt block — same structure as RU, English wording):
```
I'm a freelance designer writing a business proposal to a small coffee shop owner.
They've seen my work on Instagram but don't know my pricing or timeline.
Keep it under 150 words, no corporate tone, don't mention competitors.
Format: three short paragraphs and one concrete next step at the end.
```
9. Before/after: generic draft vs. specific draft naming the coffee shop's real details.
