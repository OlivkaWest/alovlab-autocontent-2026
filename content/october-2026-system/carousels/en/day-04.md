# Day 4 · Oct 4, 2026 · Carousel EN (@alov.lab) — «5 things you shouldn't paste into a chat»

Same 10-slide structure as `carousels/ru/day-04.md`. English text:

1. Cover: "5 THINGS YOU SHOULDN'T PASTE INTO A CHAT" / "Check your last few conversations."
2. Problem: "One innocent paste, and a client's data is sitting in someone else's service."
3. #1 Passwords & access keys — even "temporary" ones. Rotate them if you already pasted one.
4. #2 Contracts without redaction — names, amounts, party details stay out until you strip them.
5. #3 A client's financial data — it's not your data to decide to share.
6. #4 Medical data — even "just for context" in an example.
7. #5 Someone else's personal data without consent — phone, address, their messages. Not your call.
8. What to do instead: redact — "Client A" instead of a name, "N amount" instead of a sum, keep only the task structure.
9. Check yourself: open your last 10 AI chats — any of the five on that list?
10. Wrap + CTA: "Not paranoia — basic hygiene for handling other people's data." CTA: checklist card in Telegram (IG: save the post).
