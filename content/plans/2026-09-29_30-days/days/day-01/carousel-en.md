# Day 1 · Carousel (EN adaptation) — «5 ChatGPT settings that change every answer»

> Same structure and CORE IDEA as `carousel-ru.md`, natural English hooks and CTA — not a literal
> translation. Not a separate day of the plan (per instruction §9).

## Slide 1 — Cover
**Headline:** YOU'VE USED CHATGPT WRONG FOR A YEAR
**Subtext:** 5 settings that change every answer

## Slide 2 — Problem
**Headline:** One ChatGPT answers everyone the same way
**Text:** Until you set it up, it doesn't know who you are — so it gives you the average answer, the one it gives everyone else.

## Slide 3 — Principle
**Headline:** Five settings fix this
**Text:** No upgrade needed. Just five things to turn on, once.

## Slide 4 — Step 1: Custom instructions
**Headline:** 1. Tell it who you are — once
**Text:** One field in settings, read in every chat, not just the one where you wrote it.
**Copy-paste example:**
```
I'm a [role]. Keep answers short and direct, no preamble.
If you're missing information, ask — don't guess.
Explain technical terms in plain language.
```

## Slide 5 — Step 2: Memory
**Headline:** 2. Turn on memory
**Text:** With memory on, ChatGPT remembers your niche, style, ongoing projects. Without it, every chat starts from zero.

## Slide 6 — Step 3: Projects
**Headline:** 3. Group chats into projects
**Text:** One folder per task instead of thirty scattered chats. Context stops resetting between sessions.

## Slide 7 — Step 4: Match the model to the task
**Headline:** 4. You don't always need the strongest model
**Text:** Quick question — fast model, saves time. Complex multi-step task — reasoning model, saves headaches.

## Slide 8 — Step 5: Temporary chat
**Headline:** 5. Temporary chat for private stuff
**Text:** Not saved, not used for memory. Good for sensitive topics or anything you don't want it to remember.

## Slide 9 — Before / After
**Headline:** Same question. Different answer.
**Text:** Without setup, ChatGPT answers generically. With all five settings on, it answers with your context, your tone, your history.

## Slide 10 — Wrap + CTA
**Headline:** Set it up once
**Text:** Five minutes, and ChatGPT stops being a faceless robot. Full breakdown and the ready instructions template — in Telegram.
**CTA:** «Get the template in Telegram.»
